Life and work, all in order: your partner planner app.

CONCEPT

A planner that works as your partner

"Nagi" (凪) is the calm when the wind drops and the waves of the sea settle.
While your mind is restless, you cannot see what you truly want to do.
You only live once, so walk the path you truly want.
Pause, face yourself, see the way ahead, and act steadily. That is what this planner is for.

01Face yourself

First, set your own core

Face yourself and set the "values" you want to hold dear in life and the "personal rules" you decide for yourself.
Once your core is set, break your goals down for each role, such as Self, Family and Work: from 10 years ahead to 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, 3 months, this month and this week.
The values and goals you set here are linked to your daily "journal time", so you keep them in mind naturally.
Add what you want to achieve to My List with photos. Viewing them as a slideshow in each morning's journal time keeps your motivation up. In Map, you can also add places you want to visit.
Nagi Planner is full of ideas that help you move forward step by step.

What matters in life, your rules, and goals for each role from 10 years ahead to this week

02Morning journal time

Set up your day in the morning journal time

Start a pleasant day with the morning journal time.
Check your values and goals to settle your mind, then design today's schedule and actions.
You can log your body as well, and you can change the questions to suit you.

Close your eyes and breathe slowly before you begin

03Daytime

Manage everything

Daily

The menus for organizing your day are lined up in Daily (the top screen).
You can freely change which menus are shown and their order in Settings.

Change which menus are shown and their order in Settings

Schedule

Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly and Projects: many views are available. It also works with your phone's calendar.
In the Vertical view, you can add and move events and to-dos intuitively. To-dos are shown with diagonal stripes. You can also write by hand.

WeeklyMonthlyYearlyProject

Drag and drop to-dos onto the time (Mac and iPad)

Notes

Notes can be organized by notebook (folder). Templates such as meeting notes, reading notes and 1-on-1s are included.
On Mac, just drag and drop a PDF to make a PDF note, and write on it by hand.

Organize in notebooks, with checklists, tables and photos

Widgets & Apple Watch

Widgets show today's events, to-dos and routines, this week's routines and today's records. On iPad, you can also choose extra large sizes. On Apple Watch, you can check off and time to-dos and routines, and run Routine Time.

Extra large sizes too (iPhone and iPad)

04Night journal time

Just answer the questions to look back on your day

Even when you are tired at night, just answer the questions to look back without getting stuck. In the weekend, month-end, every-3-months and year-end journal times, you can see that period's records side by side and set your next goals.

NightWeekendMonth-endEvery 3 months

The night journal time (about 10 minutes) also starts with one breath

05Look back

Look back on your records as often as you like

Your records come together in your diary. Meal logs and other records go into the diary automatically, and you can write the diary by hand. You can also filter by tag.
In Insights, you can see how your routines and wake-up times change over time.
Search finds not only your diary but also what you wrote in journal time and in to-do memos.

Photos, handwriting and records, day by day
